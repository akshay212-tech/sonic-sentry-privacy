Sonic Sentry: Privacy Policy

Publisher: Chimerical Engineering (Pty) Ltd · Effective date: 5 November 2026

Summary

Sonic Sentry uses your device's microphone to analyse equipment sound on your device only. It does not record audio files, and your readings and reports stay on your device unless you share them. It has no user accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. To activate a licence, you send us a short email with your details.

Microphone

The app needs the microphone permission to learn and monitor equipment sound. It uses the microphone only while you are on the Learn or Monitoring screen, and stops it when you leave that screen. The sound is turned into frequency statistics in real time and then discarded. No raw audio is saved.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on your device:

  • the equipment details you enter (name, tag, location, notes)
  • baseline frequency statistics
  • saved readings and the anomaly event log
  • your licence key, its activation date, and the name and company you enter on the Licence screen

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes all of it.

When you create a PDF report, CSV export or data file, it is saved on your device in Documents/SonicSentry. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to share a file through Android's share sheet.

Licence activation

The app shows a device code, which is a one-way code made from your device's Android ID. It is used to tie your licence to your device. When you tap Request activation, your email app opens with a message containing your name, company and device code. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send in your email app, and we also receive the email address you send from.

We use these details only to issue and manage your licence, and we keep them in our licence records.

Data collection and sharing

Apart from the activation email you choose to send, the app does not transmit any personal or usage data to us. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties. After activation, the app does not need an internet connection to work.

Children

The app is an industrial maintenance tool and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. For questions, contact Chimerical Engineering (Pty) Ltd at chimericaleng@gmail.com.
