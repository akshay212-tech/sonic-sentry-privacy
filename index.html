Sonic Sentry: Privacy Policy

Publisher: Chimerical Engineering (Pty) Ltd · Effective date: 2 November 2026

Summary

Sonic Sentry uses your device's microphone to analyse equipment sound on your device only. It does not record audio files and does not upload or share audio or any other data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Microphone

The app needs the microphone permission to learn and monitor equipment sound. It uses the microphone only while you are on the Learn or Monitoring screen, and stops it when you leave that screen. The sound is turned into frequency statistics in real time and then discarded. No raw audio is saved.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on your device:

  • the equipment details you enter (name, tag, location, notes)
  • baseline frequency statistics
  • saved readings and the anomaly event log

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes all of it.

When you create a PDF report, CSV export or data file, it is saved on your device in Documents/SonicSentry. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to share a file through Android's share sheet.

Data collection and sharing

We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data. The app does not need an internet connection to work.

Children

The app is an industrial maintenance tool and is not directed at children.
